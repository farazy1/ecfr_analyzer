eCFR Agency Metrics

The data on this page is computed from the official eCFR API. Titles, chapters and parts are downloaded from endpoints described in the SDK documentation【360949012315245†L126-L133】 and aggregated into a set of simple metrics. Each row corresponds to a chapter (treated as an agency) with its total word count, checksum, number of versions and a custom ratio measuring how often the word “shall” appears relative to the total word count.
